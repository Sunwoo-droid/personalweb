02

Boston University RISE

Student ResearcherSummer 2025 · Boston

Built a computational model of the mBDNF/proBDNF regulatory pathway using ordinary differential equations, Hill functions, and a deep graph neural network to model neuronal survival.

Also built a brain–computer interface from a commercial EEG rig and Arduino, accepted as a late-breaking abstract at the Society for Neuroscience conference in San Diego.

Cognitive Stimuli & Adolescent Brain Function

Principal Investigator2024 — 2025 · independent

Collected EEG data and administered a self-built 40-question N-back working-memory battery, comparing exercise against short-form video consumption.

Ran the statistical analysis on both the neural and the behavioral outcomes, tracking how working memory shifted across the two conditions.
